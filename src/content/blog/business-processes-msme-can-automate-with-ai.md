## Why MSMEs benefit most

Small and medium businesses rarely have spare people. When an owner or one key employee spends hours every day on follow-ups, entries and reminders, growth stalls. AI automation gives these businesses capacity without a proportional rise in headcount. Here are ten processes where we most often find value, roughly in order of how easy they are to start.

## 1. Lead follow-up

New enquiries go cold within hours. An automation replies instantly, asks the right qualifying questions, books a call and reminds your team who to chase. Nothing slips through a busy day.

## 2. Customer questions

Opening hours, order status, pricing, how-to questions. AI answers these on WhatsApp, email or your website around the clock, in the customer's language, and passes anything unusual to a person.

## 3. Invoice processing

Supplier invoices arrive as PDFs, photos and emails. AI reads them, extracts amounts, GST details and due dates, and prepares clean entries for your accounting software for a person to approve.

## 4. Payment reminders

Late payment is a cash flow killer. Automations send polite, well-timed reminders, escalate gently and record promises to pay, so your team stops chasing manually.

## 5. Data entry from documents

Purchase orders, delivery challans, forms and certificates can be read and entered into your system automatically, with exceptions flagged for review.

## 6. Appointment and meeting scheduling

Back-and-forth over times wastes effort. An assistant finds slots, confirms, sends reminders and handles reschedules.

## 7. Reports and dashboards

Instead of someone copying numbers into a sheet each Monday, the report assembles itself from your sales, inventory and finance tools and arrives as a short summary with the points that need attention.

## 8. Resume screening and HR queries

For growing teams, AI can shortlist applications against a role, schedule interviews and answer routine employee questions about leave and policy.

## 9. Inventory and order alerts

Get a message when stock for a fast-moving item is low, a supplier is late or an order has been stuck for too long, so you act before customers notice.

## 10. Document checks

Contracts, KYC documents and compliance forms can be checked for missing fields and inconsistencies before a person spends time on them.

## How to pick from this list

Score each process on volume (how often), effort (how long each takes) and risk (cost of a mistake). Pick high volume, high effort, low risk. Put a number on it with the [AI automation ROI calculator](/ai-automations/roi-calculator/).
