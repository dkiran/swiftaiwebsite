## What an AI audit is

**An AI audit is a short, structured review that finds where AI can save your business time or money and ranks the opportunities.** It is not a technology project. It is a business exercise: where do hours go, what is repetitive, and what is it worth to fix.

## Step 1: List the recurring work (1 hour)

Ask each team lead to list everything their people do repeatedly each week. Include small tasks: sending reminders, copying data between systems, answering the same questions, preparing a weekly report. Aim for 20 to 40 items across the business.

## Step 2: Time it (1 hour)

For each item write down three numbers: how often it happens per month, how many minutes each takes and who does it. Multiply to get hours per month. Estimates are fine at this stage.

## Step 3: Add a cost (30 minutes)

Multiply hours by the approximate hourly cost of the person doing it. Now every item has a monthly rupee value. Sort by value and the top ten usually stand out.

## Step 4: Score each candidate (1 hour)

Score the top ten from 1 to 5 on four questions:

| Question | High score means |
|---|---|
| **Volume:** does it happen often? | Many times per week |
| **Rule clarity:** can we describe a good outcome? | Easy to define "done" |
| **Risk:** what if it goes wrong? | Low impact, easy to fix |
| **Data readiness:** is the input accessible? | Emails, documents or records exist digitally |

Total the scores. Highest totals are your best first projects.

## Step 5: Check feasibility (1 hour)

For your top three, ask: which tools are involved, can they connect to other software, what must a person always approve, and how would we measure success? This is where outside experience helps, because feasibility is the part most teams misjudge.

## Step 6: Choose one pilot (30 minutes)

Pick one project with a clear measure, for example "reduce first response time to enquiries from four hours to five minutes" or "cut invoice entry time by 70 percent". Set a review date four to six weeks away.

## Step 7: Estimate the return

Use your hours and costs to estimate yearly savings, then compare against the build cost. Our [AI automation ROI calculator](/ai-automations/roi-calculator/) gives you an indicative payback period.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Starting with technology.** Begin with work, not tools.
- **Choosing the most exciting idea.** Choose the most measurable one.
- **No owner.** Every pilot needs one person accountable.
- **Ignoring change management.** Tell people what is changing and why.
